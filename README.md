# MAKEVA

**BUILD · EXPLORE · CREATE · TOGETHER**

*An AI-native world-building game where players build, explore and create alongside autonomous AI.*

---

## 🌍 What is MAKEVA?

**MAKEVA** is a new kind of builder and simulation game where the world isn't simply waiting for the player to interact with it.

It can act too.

Players build, explore, create and develop their own worlds while autonomous AI characters observe what is happening, make decisions and pursue goals of their own.

The idea is simple:

> **You don't command the AI. You share the world with it.**

Your decisions influence the world.  
AI decisions influence the world.  
And what happens next isn't always predetermined.

---

## ✨ The Vision

MAKEVA combines ideas from **builder games, life simulations, management games and sandbox worlds** with autonomous AI.

The goal is to create something that is easy to understand and enjoyable to play, while allowing surprisingly complex worlds to emerge underneath.

A MAKEVA world should feel like something that is **developing with you rather than simply waiting for you**.

### 🌱 Build Your World

Create places, structures and environments and watch them become part of a living simulation.

### 🧭 Explore Without Limits

Discover worlds shaped by players, systems and AI decisions rather than following a single predetermined path.

### 👥 Create Together

MAKEVA is built around creativity rather than a single correct way to play.

### 🤖 Play with AI

AI is not just a chatbot attached to the game.

AI actors can participate in the same world, make decisions and interact with systems through controlled game actions.

### 💡 Ideas Become Reality

MAKEVA is designed as a platform for worlds that can continue expanding with new systems, content and possibilities.

## 🧠 AI as a Player

Most games use AI to control enemies, NPC behaviour or scripted events.

MAKEVA takes a different approach.

AI can become an active participant in the simulation.

Depending on the world and its available abilities, AI actors may be able to:

- Observe their surroundings
- Evaluate possible actions
- Pursue goals
- Interact with the world
- Respond to players
- Respond to other AI actors
- Build and develop
- Create unexpected situations

But AI does **not** receive unrestricted access to the game.

MAKEVA uses an authoritative action system: AI can only perform actions explicitly permitted by the simulation.

This keeps the game world deterministic, testable and under the authority of the game itself.

---

## 🎮 A Game for Different Kinds of Players

MAKEVA isn't intended to require deep knowledge of simulation games or artificial intelligence.

Someone should be able to enter a world and simply start creating.

At the same time, deeper systems can exist underneath for players who enjoy optimisation, experimentation and watching complex simulations develop.

**Small actions can create big worlds.**

## 🏗️ Development

MAKEVA is currently in active development.

The project is being built foundation-first, with emphasis on:

**Deterministic simulation · Safe AI interaction · Portable worlds · Extensible content · Long-term maintainability**

The simulation and game rules remain authoritative regardless of whether an action originates from a human player or AI.

This architecture allows AI capabilities to evolve without giving AI unrestricted control over the game itself.

---

## 🚧 Current Status

> **Early Development**

The core technical foundation has been established and development is progressing through defined milestones.

MAKEVA is **not yet a publicly playable game**.

Expect substantial changes while gameplay systems, world interaction, presentation and AI behaviour are developed.
